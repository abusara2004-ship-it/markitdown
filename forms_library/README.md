# Accounting & Administration Forms Library
## Real Estate Development Company

A comprehensive collection of professional Excel forms for accounting and administration processes, designed according to ISO 9001:2015 standards and real estate industry best practices.

---

## 📋 Forms Overview

### **1. ACCOUNTS PAYABLE & PROCUREMENT (5 Forms)**

#### 01_Vendor_Registration_Form.xlsx
- **Purpose:** Vendor onboarding and qualification
- **Key Sections:**
  - Vendor information (ID, name, business type)
  - Contact details and address
  - Banking information for payments
  - Approval tracking
- **Users:** Procurement, Finance
- **Frequency:** As needed for new vendors

#### 02_Purchase_Requisition.xlsx
- **Purpose:** Request approval for purchases
- **Key Sections:**
  - Requestor and department information
  - Item details (description, quantity, estimated cost)
  - Cost center allocation
  - Approval workflow with signature lines
- **Users:** Department heads, Procurement
- **Frequency:** For each purchase requirement

#### 03_Purchase_Order.xlsx
- **Purpose:** Formal purchase commitment to vendor
- **Key Sections:**
  - PO number and reference tracking
  - Vendor details and contact information
  - Line items with pricing
  - Terms & conditions
  - Approval sign-offs
- **Users:** Procurement, Finance
- **Frequency:** Upon PR approval

#### 04_Goods_Received_Note.xlsx
- **Purpose:** Document receipt of goods from vendors
- **Key Sections:**
  - GRN number and PO reference
  - Received items with quantities
  - Inspection and acceptance checklist
  - Discrepancy logging
  - Verification sign-offs
- **Users:** Warehouse, Receiving team
- **Frequency:** Upon delivery

#### 05_Expense_Claim_Form.xlsx
- **Purpose:** Reimburse employee business expenses
- **Key Sections:**
  - Employee identification
  - Expense categories and dates
  - Vendor and amount details
  - Attachment checklist (receipts, invoices)
  - Approval workflow
- **Users:** Employees, Managers, Finance
- **Frequency:** Monthly or as incurred

## 🎯 Usage Guidelines

### General Instructions:
1. **Save a copy** for each transaction or period
2. **Fill in all required fields** (marked in bold)
3. **Provide complete documentation** with attachments
4. **Follow approval hierarchy** as indicated
5. **Keep records** for audit and compliance purposes
6. **Use consistent formats** for dates and currency

### Best Practices:
- **Data Entry:** Ensure accuracy before submission
- **Approvals:** Obtain timely sign-offs to avoid delays
- **Storage:** Archive forms according to retention policy (typically 7 years)
- **Security:** Protect sensitive financial information
- **Version Control:** Use only the latest version of each form

### Integration with ERP/Accounting System:
- Key forms integrate with QuickBooks, SAP, or Oracle
- Use data fields compatible with your accounting software
- Export reports from these forms for system entry
- Maintain clear audit trail from form to ledger entry

## 🔐 Internal Controls & Segregation of Duties

### Key Control Points:
- **3-Way Matching:** PO ↔ GRN ↔ Invoice
- **Approval Hierarchy:** Based on transaction amount
- **Segregation of Duties:** Requestor ≠ Approver ≠ Payer
- **Audit Trail:** All forms include date, preparer, and approver fields
- **Authorization Limits:**
  - < OMR 5,000: Single approval
  - OMR 5,000 - OMR 50,000: Two approvals
  - > OMR 50,000: Director/CFO approval

## 🔄 Workflow Integration

### Typical Process Flows:

**Purchase-to-Pay Process:**
```
Purchase Requisition
        ↓
    (Approved by Manager/Finance)
        ↓
    Purchase Order
        ↓
    (Sent to Vendor)
        ↓
    Goods Received Note
        ↓
    (3-Way Match Validation)
        ↓
    Payment Authorization
        ↓
    (Bank Transfer/Check)
```

**Order-to-Cash Process:**
```
Customer Application
        ↓
    (KYC Verification)
        ↓
    Booking Form
        ↓
    (Payment Schedule Initiation)
        ↓
    Receipt Voucher (Per Installment)
        ↓
    (Customer Account Statement)
        ↓
    (Collections/Dunning if needed)
```
