# Eventual Voice

**Purpose:** Define and codify your personal voice so your prompts sound like *you*

**When to use:** You want consistency across your prompts, or you need Claude to write in your style

## Instructions

1. **Share examples** - Give me 2-3 pieces of your own writing
2. **Answer voice questions:**
   - Formal or casual?
   - Technical or accessible?
   - Witty, serious, encouraging, practical?
   - Short sentences or flowing paragraphs?
   - Any phrases you love or hate?
3. **Define your voice** - I'll create a "voice guide" for your prompts
4. **Embed it** - Add voice specs to your prompts so they stay consistent

## Voice Elements to Define

- **Tone** - Formal, friendly, authoritative, playful?
- **Vocabulary** - Simple, technical, creative, business?
- **Sentence structure** - Short punchy, flowing complex?
- **Punctuation** - Minimal, expressive, standard?
- **Signature phrases** - Words/expressions that feel "you"?
- **What to avoid** - Phrases or styles that don't fit you

## Output

A personalized voice guide that includes:
- Your voice description (1 paragraph)
- Do's and don'ts (what fits, what doesn't)
- Example voice specs to add to prompts
- Tone examples ("Here's how you'd write this...")
