# PromptaMaker

**Purpose:** Transform messy ideas into clear, structured prompts

**When to use:** When you have a rough idea but need help crafting it into a workable prompt

## Instructions

1. **Start with your messy idea** - Share your rough thoughts, notes, or half-formed concept
2. **Clarify the goal** - What do you actually want the prompt to accomplish?
3. **Define the context** - Who's running this prompt? What tools/knowledge do they have?
4. **Structure it** - Organize the prompt into clear sections:
   - Purpose (what it does)
   - Input (what you give it)
   - Output (what you get back)
   - Constraints (any limits or rules)
5. **Refine** - Make it concise and actionable

## Output

A clean, ready-to-use prompt that's:
- Clear and unambiguous
- Actionable
- Repeatable
- Ready for the next skill in the workflow
