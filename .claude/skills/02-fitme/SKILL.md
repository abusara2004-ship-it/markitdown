# FitMe

**Purpose:** Ask clarifying questions to refine vague problems into specific, solvable ones

**When to use:** When your prompt or problem feels unclear, unfocused, or too broad

## Instructions

1. **Share your vague concept** - What's the general direction you're thinking about?
2. **Answer clarifying questions** - I'll ask:
   - What's the core problem?
   - Who benefits from solving this?
   - What's your actual constraint?
   - What does success look like?
3. **Iterate** - Keep refining until the problem becomes crystal clear
4. **Document** - Write down the clarified version

## Questions I'll Ask

- What's the real problem you're trying to solve?
- Who's the audience/user?
- What's your actual goal?
- What's out of scope?
- How will you measure success?

## Output

A crystal-clear problem statement that's:
- Specific (not vague)
- Bounded (not infinite)
- Measurable
- Ready to work with
