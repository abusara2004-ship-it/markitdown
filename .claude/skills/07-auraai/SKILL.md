# AuraAI

**Purpose:** Refine tone, style, and personality—make output sound right

**When to use:** Your prompt works, but the output tone/feel isn't quite right

## Instructions

1. **Share the output** - Paste what you got from your prompt
2. **Describe the issue** - "Too formal," "Not witty enough," "Needs more detail," etc.
3. **Define the target tone:**
   - Professional vs. casual
   - Detailed vs. concise
   - Encouraging vs. blunt
   - Creative vs. practical
4. **I'll adjust** - Rewrite your prompt to achieve the right tone

## Tone Adjustments

**Formality Scale**
- Extremely casual → Friendly → Balanced → Professional → Extremely formal

**Energy Level**
- Understated → Neutral → Upbeat → Enthusiastic → Over-the-top

**Complexity**
- Dumbed down → Simple → Balanced → Technical → PhD-level

**Personality**
- None → Slight → Moderate → Strong → Overwhelming

## Output

Revised prompt instructions that will produce:
- The right tone in responses
- Appropriate formality level
- Matching your intended personality
- Consistent style throughout
