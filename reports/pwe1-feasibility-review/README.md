# PWE1 HOSS™-VWP Feasibility Review

Feasibility assessment of PWE1's HOSS™-VWP modular thermal produced-water treatment
system, prepared for PDO / Oxy technology-partnership evaluation.

## Contents

- **`feasibility-review.html`** — Full written feasibility review: technology
  overview, market fit, technical assessment, commercial viability, risk
  analysis, due-diligence roadmap, and recommendation.
- **`presentation-deck.html`** — 19-slide presentation version of the same
  findings, for a boardroom walkthrough. Navigate with arrow keys, scroll,
  or the on-screen controls.

## Summary

**Recommendation:** Qualified proceed to pilot. PWE1's HOSS™-VWP addresses a
real operational cost (produced-water disposal) with a plausible value
proposition (recovered steam, water, and minerals) for PDO/Oxy heavy-oil and
high-water-cut fields. All performance figures are currently vendor claims
and require independent pilot validation and written performance guarantees
before any capital commitment.

**Confidence:** 8/10 — strong synthesis of publicly available vendor
material; independent technical and commercial evidence is outstanding.

Source: PWE public technology page (pwe1.co), reviewed August 2026.
